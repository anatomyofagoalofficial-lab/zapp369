The proof · why the honest engine is the safe one

The Maths. Why it settles at two and never explodes.

1 + ½ + ¼ + ⅛ + … = 2

Add a half, then a quarter, then an eighth, forever, and it never explodes. It settles, on exactly two. Recycle a share of the value each cycle and the long run effect is 1 ÷ (1 − recycled share). Recycle half, and it is exactly two. The engine roughly doubles real value, then holds.

The quiet truth

The series settles only because the recycled share is less than one. The part that is not recycled, the share that pays the workers and helps families, is exactly what keeps it from blowing up. Pay the people and the maths stays beautiful. Promise the moon and it never settles.

The engine · two drains, one buyback

The Double Burn. Fewer coins, deeper market.

Each cycle the buyback buys ⚡ZAPP on the open market. Then each coin goes down one of two drains, and the community chooses the mix.

Why two and not one

The burn lifts the floor. The locked liquidity makes the market strong so the price can hold near that floor. Backed and stable at once.

Never pulled

What goes into liquidity stays there. What goes to the burn address is gone forever. Both are public wallets anyone can watch, every cycle, on chain.
