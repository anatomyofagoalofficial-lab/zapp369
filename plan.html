Section 07 · the proof

Why it sums to two

The honest engine is the safe one, and a simple piece of maths proves it.

1 + 1/2 + 1/4 + 1/8 + … = 2

Add a half, then a quarter, then an eighth, forever, and it never explodes. It settles, on exactly two. Infinite in steps, finite in result. Our engine works the same way. Recycle a share of the value each cycle and the long run effect is one divided by one minus that share. Recycle half, and it is exactly two. The engine roughly doubles real value, then holds, strong and steady.

long run effect = 1 ÷ (1 − recycled share) · recycle 1/2 → ×2
The quiet truth. The series settles because the recycled share is less than one. The part that pays the workers and helps families is exactly what keeps it strong and steady. Pay the people and the maths stays beautiful.
