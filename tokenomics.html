The double burn

Bought coins go down one of two drains. Burned to null, gone forever, so the floor rises. Or locked into liquidity, so the market gets deeper and can never be pulled. The community chooses the mix.

Why it does not explode

1 + ½ + ¼ + ⅛ and so on settles at 2. Recycle a share each cycle and it converges rather than running away. The part that is not recycled, paying people and funding the cause, is exactly what keeps the arithmetic honest.

Paid for by real revenue

The engine is fed by profit from the shop and from work done, not by new buyers paying out old ones. That is the whole difference, and it is why the split above has wages in it.
