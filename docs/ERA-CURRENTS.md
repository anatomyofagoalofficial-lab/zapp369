# Era Currents — the ⚡ZAPP generative philosophy · 3 · 6 · 9

A meticulously crafted algorithmic movement in three tenses. Every animated system on
zapp369.energy obeys one law: **the function must belong to its era.** Nothing modern may
move in 1901. Nothing antique may tick in the now. The code is the time machine.

**3 · PAST — Candle Physics (1901).** Light behaves as it did before electricity was tamed:
volumetric god-rays from a single off-frame source, gold dust rising on convection, embers,
and a wireframe Wardenclyffe whose discharge is built by recursive midpoint displacement —
the same jagged geometry as a real spark gap. Ground pulses spread through the Earth, as
Tesla claimed. Palette: candle gold on espresso dark. Allowed motion: drift, flicker, discharge.
Forbidden: easing curves that feel digital, neon, UI chrome. Every constant here was tuned
by hand across dozens of iterations — the discharge probability (0.55), the ray count (7),
the ember population (14) are not defaults, they are decisions.

**6 · PRESENT — Paper Stillness (now).** The present is the only era with no generative canvas
at all — that is its algorithm. Flat parchment, a fine 64px ledger grid, live numbers ticking
(price, market cap, nine world clocks at one-second resolution). The only animation permitted
is *data being alive*. Stillness as craft: the restraint of a master who knows when not to draw.

**9 · FUTURE — Signal Rain (what comes).** Deterministic chaos in brand gold: a throttled
22fps digital rain of katakana, 3-6-9 and ZAPP glyphs; a fibonacci-sphere Earth whose surface
points are tiny ⚡ bolts; energy packets travelling great-circle arcs between towers; and a
live chart whose gold line is drawn from the real pump.fun pool — the future rendered as a
frequency that is already climbing. Allowed motion: rain, orbit, transmission, tick.

**∞ · HUB — The Space Between.** Outside time, the one place the brand palette opens: a
three-armed spiral galaxy (the arms are the 3, the 6, the 9), purple-and-cyan starlight,
connection streams of 80 fine pulsing dots arcing between eras, and a 0.8-second warp that
blooms to white. Seeded by the clock, governed by the 9-second beat (`BEAT = 9.0`).

**Craft law.** Every system must read as the product of deep computational expertise:
shadowBlur is banned (additive double-strokes instead), pixel ratios are capped, particle
counts are chosen, never guessed. If a parameter cannot be defended, it is removed. Beauty
lives in the process — the page is never twice the same, yet always unmistakably ZAPP.
